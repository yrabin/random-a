# Part 1 — For students

## 1. Getting started

1. Open the app link your parent gives you. On a tablet or phone, choose **Share → Add to Home Screen** (iPad/iPhone) or **⋮ → Install app** (Android/Chrome) so it opens like a normal app and works offline.
2. Ollie the otter 🦦 asks for a **nickname** and your **level (P4/P5/P6)**. Use a nickname — please don't type your full name, school or address anywhere.
3. You are ready! Everything is saved **on this device** automatically.

The bottom bar (or top bar on a computer) takes you to **Home, Maths, English, Science, Ask Ollie (AI)** and **More**.

## 2. The Home page

* **Level bar** – XP you earn for answering questions. Every level has a fun title (Otter Pup → … → Legend of the Lion City).
* **Countdown** – set your PSLE date to see how many days are left.
* **Daily Challenge** – 6 mixed questions (3 Maths, 1 Grammar, 1 Science, 1 Vocabulary). It's the same set for everyone on the same day, and finishing it gives **+30 bonus XP**.
* **Ollie suggests** – after you've tried a topic a few times, Ollie points you to your weakest Maths topic.
* **Tip of the day** – a quick exam tip.

## 3. Maths

Tap **Maths**, choose your level, then pick one or more topics (or none for a mixed set), choose 5–20 questions and a number size (Easier / Normal / Challenge), and press **Start**.

**28 topics** from the P4–P6 syllabus, including: money & multi-step problems, LCM/HCF, factors & multiples, fractions (add, subtract, remainder, ×, ÷), decimals, angles, area & perimeter, time, number patterns, percentage (discount & 9% GST), percentage change, ratio (including *before & after*), average, rate, triangles, volume of cuboids, speed, algebra (simplify, solve, substitute), circles, age problems, and the heuristics *assumption* (chickens & rabbits) and *equal after giving*.

**Every question is freshly made**, so you never run out – and each one has complete working.

**How to answer**
* Type your number. Fractions: `3/4`. Mixed numbers: `1 3/4`. Decimals: `2.5`. Money: `12.50` (no need for `$`). You can type units — they are ignored.
* Algebra answers look like `5a + 3` (any order is fine).
* Press **Enter** or **Check ✔**. Stuck? Press **💡 Hint** first.
* Fractions should be in **simplest form**. If the value is right but not simplified, Ollie still counts it but reminds you.
* After you answer you see the **step-by-step working**. If you answered wrong, the question is saved to your **Mistake Notebook**.

**⚡ 60-second Blitz** – mental maths speed round (add, subtract, times tables, division, percentages). Your answer is checked as you type. Beat your personal best!

## 4. English

| Activity | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Vocabulary** | 67 PSLE-level words: choose the meaning, or choose the word that fits a sentence. Browse them all in **Word list** (🔊 hear the word). |
| **Grammar** | 46 questions across 14 topics — subject-verb agreement, tenses, articles, prepositions, pronouns, conjunctions, word forms, comparatives, phrasal verbs, quantifiers, punctuation, passive voice, reported speech, question tags. Every answer has a reason. |
| **Spot the Singlish** | Choose the standard-English sentence. Singlish is fine with friends — but use standard English in exams, compositions and orals! |
| **Synthesis & Transformation** | Rewrite/combine sentences. Type your answer, compare with the model, and mark yourself honestly. Other correct answers are possible. |
| **Idioms** | Meaning and use of 22 common idioms. |
| **Composition Lab** | Writing prompts; a **plot-twist generator** (who/where/problem/twist/lesson); a **Show, don't tell** feelings bank; and a **free checker** that looks at your word count, paragraphs, sentence length variety, repeated sentence starters, weak words (said, very, nice, went…), "telling" phrases, Singlish and missing dialogue/senses/ending. |
| **Oral practice** | Stimulus-based conversation topics with tips, a self-check list, 🎤 voice typing (if your browser supports it) and Read-Aloud passages you can 🔊 listen to. |

## 5. Science

* **Quizzes by MOE theme**: Diversity, Cycles, Systems, Energy, Interactions (58 questions, each with an explanation) – or a mixed quiz. Press **A–D** or **1–4** on a keyboard to answer.
* **Open-ended (CER)** – write your answer, then **Show model answer**. Ollie highlights the **key words** you used and the ones you missed. Be honest and press ✅ or ❌.
* **Keyword cards** – flip through must-know terms (evaporation, conductor, producer…).
* **The CER trick**: **C**laim (answer the question) → **E**vidence (use the data in the question) → **R**easoning (link to the science idea: "This is because…").

## 6. Chinese corner

* **成语 quiz** and **词语 quiz** with pinyin; **成语 reading list** with example sentences (例句).
* This is light revision to go with your school Chinese lessons, not a replacement. (For Malay or Tamil, the AI tutor can help if your parent has set it up.)

## 7. Mistake Notebook

Every wrong answer is saved automatically. Ollie brings each one back after **1, 2, 4, 8, 16 and 32 days** (a method called *spaced repetition*). Answer it right at each review and it becomes **mastered** 🏅. Answer wrong and it goes back to the start.

➡ Spend **5 minutes a day** here. It's the highest-value habit in the whole app.

## 8. PSLE AL Calculator & Planner

* Enter your marks (0–100) for **English, Mother Tongue, Maths, Science**.
* You see each subject's **AL (1–8)** and your **total (4–32)**. Lower is better. AL bands: 90+ = AL1, 85–89 = AL2, 80–84 = AL3, 75–79 = AL4, 65–74 = AL5, 45–64 = AL6, 20–44 = AL7, below 20 = AL8.
* **Target planner** – slide to your goal total; the planner shows the **cheapest** way to reach it (e.g. "English: 84 → 85 marks is only +1 mark to move AL3 → AL2").
* **Save to my score history** after each prelim or practice paper to draw a trend line.
* Set the **countdown** date here too.

> This is a study aid following the AL system for 2021 onwards. Posting groups and special rules (e.g. Higher Mother Tongue adjustments, tie-breakers) can change; always confirm with MOE/SEAB and your school.

## 9. Progress, XP, streaks and badges

* **XP**: +10 for a correct answer, +2 for trying, with a bonus for answering several in a row (combo 🔥).
* **Streak**: practise on consecutive days. Miss a day and it resets – so even 5 minutes counts.
* **16 badges**: First splash, Wave rider, Lightning (10 correct in a row), Week warrior (7-day streak), Mistake mender, All-rounder, AI explorer, Storyteller, Deep focus…
* **Progress page**: 4-week activity calendar, accuracy by subject, **topic heat-map** (green ≥ 80%, yellow 55–79%, red < 55%), and a **Print for parents** button.

## 10. Focus timer

Choose 15 + 3, 25 + 5 or 40 + 10 minutes (focus + break). A finished focus session gives XP equal to the minutes and counts towards the **Deep focus** badge. Put your phone away, pick **one** topic, and go.

## 11. Printable worksheets

**More → Worksheets**: choose level, number of questions and (optionally) topics. Press **Print** or *Save as PDF*. A new page with the **answer key** (optionally with working) is included. The **Sheet code** reproduces the exact same worksheet later – ask a friend to type your code to get the same one!

## 12. Tips for answering

1. Underline the numbers and what the question asks. 2. Draw a bar model. 3. Write units. 4. Check by putting your answer back into the story.
5. In Science, use CER. 6. In compositions, *show* feelings. 7. Little and often beats cramming — and sleep helps your brain remember.

# Part 2 — For parents

**What it is:** a revision companion, not a replacement for school or tuition. It gives unlimited, correct-by-construction Maths practice with working, plus English/Science/Chinese revision, a PSLE score planner and habit tools.

**Privacy**
* No accounts, ads, trackers or cookies. The server only serves web pages — it doesn't receive your child's answers.
* Progress is stored **in the browser on that device** (local storage). Clearing site data erases it, so use **Settings → Download backup** monthly. **Restore backup** moves progress to another device.
* Several children can share one device with **Settings → Profiles**.
* The microphone button uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. Depending on the browser (e.g. Chrome), the audio may be processed by the browser vendor's cloud service; skip the 🎤 button if you're not comfortable.

**Progress reports**
* **Progress → Print for parents** gives a one-page summary (streak, accuracy, heat-map, badges). With AI enabled, **Weekly parent update** writes a short plain-language summary from *anonymous* statistics (no name is sent).
* The red topics in the heat-map are where a short chat ("explain this to me like I'm the teacher") helps most.

**Healthy use**
* Ask your child to explain the working of one question aloud each day.
* Focus timers help; ask them to put the device in "Do not disturb".
* Daily Challenge + 5 minutes of Mistake Notebook + 1 topic practice ≈ 20 minutes — enough to build a strong habit.

# Part 3 — Setting up AI helpers (optional)

**What you get:** 🤖 **Tutor chat** (hints first), **Question Forge** (new questions on any topic, multiple-choice, open-ended or a full comprehension passage), **Snap & Check** (photo of handwritten working → line-by-line feedback), **Composition Coach** (PSLE-style content/language scores, rewrites, vocabulary upgrades, grammar fixes), **Situational Writing** marking, **Oral Coach**, **AI marking of open-ended answers**, and **Coach's note / parent update**. A **🤖 Explain with AI** button also appears after each question.

**"BYOK"** = *bring your own key*. You create an account with an AI company, create a secret **API key**, and paste it into the app. You pay the AI company directly for what you use (typically cents per month for a student on a small model). **The app has no AI of its own and no server-side key**, so without a key all the AI buttons stay locked and everything else works.

### Step by step (about 5 minutes)

1. **Choose a provider** (any one):
   | Provider | Where to get a key | Notes |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Anthropic (Claude)** | <https://console.anthropic.com/settings/keys> | Default model `claude-haiku-5-5` (smallest/cheapest). Supports photos. |
   | **OpenAI** | <https://platform.openai.com/api-keys> | Default `gpt-4o-mini`. Supports photos. |
   | **Google Gemini** | <https://aistudio.google.com/apikey> | Has a free tier with limits. Supports photos. |
   | **OpenRouter** | <https://openrouter.ai/keys> | One key, many models; some are free (names ending `:free`). Photos depend on the model. |
   | **Custom / local** | — | Any OpenAI-compatible server, e.g. **Ollama** on a home computer (`http://localhost:11434/v1`, model `llama3.2`), LM Studio or Groq. $0 if local. |
2. **Parent: create the key.** Add a small prepaid balance or set a **monthly spending limit** (e.g. US$5) in the provider's billing page. Create a key used *only* for this app so it can be revoked on its own.
3. In the app: **Settings → AI helper (optional)**: pick the provider, paste the key, optionally change the model name, and press **Save & test**. You should see "✅ Works!".
4. Go to **Ask Ollie** and try *Tutor chat*.

### Safety & cost tips
* The key is saved **only in this browser** and is sent only to the provider you chose (directly from the browser). **Backups don't include the key.** Use **Remove key** before lending the device to someone else.
* Only use it on devices you trust; don't paste it on a shared/public computer.
* A small model is plenty for P4–P6. A typical question or explanation uses a few thousand tokens (a fraction of a cent). Photos and long compositions cost a little more.
* Ollie's instructions: hints before answers, simple words, never ask for personal information, stay on schoolwork. Still — **AI can be wrong.** If an explanation looks odd, check with a teacher or parent. Generated questions especially should be spot-checked.
* For **Custom/local** servers from an HTTPS site, browsers block plain `http://` calls (mixed content). Use the app on `http://localhost:8080` or serve your local AI over HTTPS.

# Sample 4-week plan

*20–30 minutes a day, 6 days a week. Adjust to your child's level and schedule.*

| Week | Focus | Daily routine |
|---|---|---|
| **1 – Foundations** | Find weak spots | Daily Challenge → 10 mixed Maths → 5 min Mistake Notebook. Set the PSLE countdown and target AL. |
| **2 – Depth** | Fix the red topics | Daily Challenge → 10 Qs on the weakest Maths topic → 1 English activity (grammar/vocab) → Notebook. |
| **3 – Writing & Science** | Compositions, open-ended | Daily Challenge → 1 composition (free check; AI coach if enabled) → Science open-ended ×3 → Notebook. Print a worksheet for the weekend. |
| **4 – Exam mode** | Timed practice | Print a 15–20 question worksheet and time it; mark with the answer key; log the paper in the PSLE planner; Notebook; oral practice (read aloud + 1 stimulus). |

Weekly: look at the **Progress** page together for 5 minutes, celebrate the streak, and pick next week's red topic.

# FAQ & troubleshooting

**Do I need the internet?** Only to load the app the first time and for AI features. After that, practice works offline.

**Where did my progress go?** It's stored in the browser. A different browser/device, a private window, or cleared site data shows an empty app. Restore from a backup.

**Can two kids share the app?** Yes — **Settings → Add profile**.

**Are the Maths answers always right?** Each question and its working are calculated by the program, and the test-suite re-checks every topic against hundreds of random examples. Report any mistakes via the GitHub repository.

**The AI said something wrong.** AI can make errors. Ask a teacher, and use *Snap & Check* or *Explain with AI* as a second opinion rather than the final word.

**My fraction answer was marked "unsimplified".** The value is correct, but PSLE expects simplest form (e.g. `1/2` not `2/4`).

**Sound is annoying.** Settings → Appearance & sound → turn off *Sound effects*.

**Dark mode?** Settings → Theme (Auto follows the device).

**Can I use it without Docker?** Yes — if you can open the website, that's all you need. Docker is only for whoever hosts it (see the [deployment guide](DEPLOYMENT.md)).
